About this data
NASA Sentry impact-risk catalogue.

Palermo scale: compares impact probability to the "background" rate of random impacts. Palermo < 0 means less risky than background. Most objects here have Palermo < −2 (100× below background).

JPL's own caveat: probabilities can be inaccurate by a factor of 10 or more — orbits are still being refined.

Apophis appeared on this list for years. Better observations confirmed zero risk through 2116 — it was removed. Removal is the best possible outcome.
